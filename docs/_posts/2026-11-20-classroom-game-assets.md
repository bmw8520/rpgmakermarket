## What "classroom game assets" really means

The phrase covers three different things, and mixing them up is how teachers end up with a folder of files they cannot use.

1. **Game-engine assets**: plugins, minigames, sprites, tilesheets, music and sound generators you drop into a project in an engine such as RPG Maker. You get a real, playable game.
2. **Lesson templates**: slide decks, spreadsheets and documents with game rules built in. You get an activity, not software.
3. **Game adaptations**: tabletop games reworked for a class period. You get a ruleset and printable materials.

If you are an indie developer, a hobbyist, an educator or a small studio working in RPG Maker, category one is where you build. Categories two and three are where you borrow ideas for what to build. The rest of this article compares real options in each, with the engine option first.

## Purdue's classroom adaptations

Purdue's science outreach page, [Game-based Learning in the Science Classroom](https://www.purdue.edu/science/K12/games/index.html), lists tabletop games reworked for school use. Periodic: A Game of the Elements and Subatomic: An Atom Building Game are each designed for a 45-minute class period. The page also lists Wingspan: The Classroom Adaptation, Rock Collector, Photosynthesis, The Shipwreck Arcana, Pandemic, Apollo: NASA Moon Mission, Gravity Maze and Higher or Lower.

These are a strong source of design ideas. A 45-minute limit is a useful target for your own game too: it tells you how long one play session should be. If you want to turn an element-matching idea like Periodic into a digital game, our STEM minigame modules are the natural starting point, because they already run inside RPG Maker.

## NSW Edu Game Templates

The New South Wales Government publishes [Edu Game Templates](https://sites.google.com/education.nsw.gov.au/gamification/home/gamification-in-the-classroom/edu-game-templates) as Google Slides, including a Memory Game and Interactive Diagrams. Educators may copy and adapt them. They are shared under a Creative Commons 4.0 attribution licence and are not to be sold for profit.

That last condition matters. If you are a teacher making a lesson for your own class, it is fine. If you are a small studio wanting to package content as a product, a no-sale template is the wrong base. Whitelabel-ready assets from our library are meant for that commercial case.

## Ánimo Games: Star Stuff Edu

[Star Stuff Edu](https://animogames.org/educators) is a free browser-friendly programming puzzle game for ages 10 and up, with 70 curated levels across 11 sessions. ## How to customize RPG Maker assets for a lesson

Customization is the second half of this keyword, and it is simpler than most teachers expect.

1. **Pick the module, not the whole game.** Choose a plugin or minigame that matches one learning goal: matching, sorting, route-finding, a timed quiz.
2. **Set parameters in the Plugin Manager.** MZ includes a plugin manager where each plugin lists its settings. Questions, labels and values usually live there, so you change content without touching code.
3. **Swap the art.** Import your own sprites or tilesheets through the engine's resource tools. Our sprite and tilesheet bundles give you a consistent set to start from.
4. **Replace the sound.** Generate loop-ready background music and effects with our generators, so the mood fits the unit: calm for reading, tense for a timed round.
5. **Test with a student.** Hand the game to one learner with no instructions. If they cannot start in two minutes, simplify the first screen.

Because our plugins are built to be added one at a time, you can start with a single minigame and grow the project over a term.

## How to check classroom suitability

The Institute of Play describes its games as teacher-tested and student-approved, designed with educators. You can borrow that standard for any asset:

- **Time fit.** Can one play session finish inside a class period? Purdue's adaptations aim at 45 minutes.
- **Ease of start.** Can a student begin without reading a manual?
- **Content control.** Can you change the questions and the vocabulary to your curriculum?
- **Accessibility.** Are text size, color contrast and controls adjustable? Keep text short and avoid color-only cues.
- **Device fit.** Will it run on the school machines you actually have?

## Licenses: what you may change, share and sell

This is where most classroom projects go wrong. Creative Commons tags are short, but each letter is a rule:

- **BY** means you must credit the creator.
- **NC** means no commercial use.
- **ND** means no derivatives: you may not edit it.
- **SA** means share any changes under the same license.

So CC BY-NC-ND, as on Star Stuff Edu, lets you use and share but not edit or sell. The NSW templates allow editing but not selling. If you want to edit and sell, look at assets under plain CC BY or CC0 licenses. itch.io lets you browse [assets under Creative Commons Attribution](https://www.itch.io/games/assets-cc4-by/), which allow adaptation with credit. Whichever you choose, keep a short credits file in your project listing each asset and its license.

Our whitelabel-ready resources are made for commercial use or resale, which is the clearest path for a small studio selling an educational RPG.

## FAQ

### Can I use RPG Maker to make educational games without coding?

Yes. Our plugins are modular, so you add a capability such as a minigame or an animated travel scene and adjust its settings in the plugin manager. Most classroom projects need only a few plugins and your own questions.

### Can I sell an educational game that uses these assets?

Our resources include whitelabel-ready options for commercial use or resale. For any outside asset, read its license first: a non-commercial tag, as on Star Stuff Edu, rules out selling.

### Which Creative Commons license is easiest for classroom adaptation?

CC BY and CC0 allow editing. CC BY-NC-ND does not allow changes. NC means no sale, so a school-only project can use it but a commercial product cannot.

### How long should a classroom game session be?

Aim for one class period. Purdue's classroom adaptations, such as Periodic and Subatomic, are each designed for 45 minutes, which is a good ceiling for a single play session.

### Does RPG Maker MZ come with assets I can start from?

Yes. MZ ships with a large built-in set of tilesets, character sprites, battlers and title screens, according to [ModDB's engine listing](https://www.moddb.com/engines/rpg-maker-mz). Our sprite bundles and plugins extend that starting set.
